What is CIR-Engine 503M and how does its Causal Transformer architecture work?

CIR-Engine 503M is an open-source, custom 12-layer decoder-only Causal Transformer model designed by Julio Cesar, featuring 503 Million parameters, RoPE positional encoding, Grouped-Query Attention (GQA), and a deterministic graph verification layer that prevents hallucinations.

  • 503 Million dense parameters across 12 causal transformer decoder layers.
  • Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) mapping 16 Query heads to 4 Key/Value heads (4:1 KV compression).

  • Sustained ~6,200 tokens/sec throughput during distributed PyTorch DDP Ring-AllReduce training.
